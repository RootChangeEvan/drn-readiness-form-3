With the 2026 election period approaching, this self-assessment is designed to help each DRN take a practical look at its current readiness. The goal is to identify what is already in place, where there may still be gaps, and where additional support could be useful.

Each DRN can decide who should complete the assessment. In some cases, one person may be best positioned to respond. In others, several people involved in the DRN’s work may want to complete it separately or discuss the questions together.

Please answer based on what is actually in place today. The self-assessment should take about 8–10 minutes.

Responses will be used to support follow-up conversations with The Carter Center and Root Change. They are not intended to rank or score DRNs against one another.

Key terms
Response
Any deliberate DRN action, including communicating, convening, connecting partners, activating trusted messengers, or escalating information to another actor.
Trusted messenger
A person or organization with established credibility with a specific audience the DRN may need to reach.
Partner
An organization or individual outside the DRN that may play a role in election-period response.
Election period
The period from early voting through certification.
Tested
The process, relationship, or capability has been used in a real situation or practiced through a scenario or exercise closely enough to confirm that the people involved know what to do and that it works as intended.
